      What formats work?

      Calendar (.ics): export from Google Calendar (Settings → Import & export), Outlook or Apple Calendar. Repeating weekly/daily events become repeating items; past events are skipped.

      CSV: a header row with title and any of date, days, start, end, notes, priority. days can be Mon,Wed,Fri, weekdays or daily. Rows with neither date nor days happen every day.

      Text: one item per line: optional day or date, a colon, a time or time range, then the title. A line that is just a day name (“Monday:”) applies to the lines under it.
