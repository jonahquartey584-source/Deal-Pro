# Deal Pro homepage tutorial: narration script

Voice: ElevenLabs "Adam". Length: about 3 minutes 30 seconds.
Each scene lists what the voiceover says, then what should be on screen.
The Deal Community has its own tutorial, so it is only introduced here.

---

## 1. Welcome (0:00)

**Voiceover:**
Hi, and welcome to Deal Pro. Deal Pro helps you find UK property deals, check whether the numbers really work, and keep everything organised in one place. In the next few minutes I'll show you how to sign in, choose how deeply the AI works, find live deals, analyse them properly, and share deals with other sourcers.

**On screen:** Home page. Cursor rests on the headline "Property deals, found and analysed."

---

## 2. Signing in (0:20)

**Voiceover:**
Start by clicking Sign in, at the top right. You can continue with Google in one click, or use your email and a password. If you ever forget your password, click "Forgot password?", and we'll email you a link to set a new one. Once you're in, you'll stay signed in on that device for thirty days, so you don't have to log in every time you visit.

**On screen:** Click **Sign in**. Hover **Continue with Google**. Type an email and password, click **Sign in**. Briefly hover **Forgot password?** before signing in.

---

## 3. Power levels (0:45)

**Voiceover:**
Before you search or analyse, choose a power level. You'll see three options.
Scout, in green, gives you a fast first look, and uses the least credits.
Analyst, in orange, gives you a full analysis, and uses more credits.
Expert, in red, is the most thorough. It searches deeper, checks the details and stress-tests the numbers, and it uses the most credits.
On the Free plan you can use Scout. Analyst and Expert come with Premium and Max. Your credits left this week are shown just below the options.

**On screen:** Open **Deal Analyser**. Click **Scout**, then **Analyst**, then **Expert**, pausing on each so its colour and "Uses … credits" line are visible. Hover the "credits left this week" line.

---

## 4. Finding deals with Deal Finder (1:10)

**Voiceover:**
Now open Deal Finder. On the left, set your filters: whether you want to rent or buy, the number of bedrooms, your price range, the location, and the property type. You can also choose furnished or unfurnished, and private landlords only.
Then tick the sites you want to search: OpenRent, SpareRoom, Gumtree, Rightmove, Zoopla and Facebook Marketplace. If you're looking for commercial property, there's a separate commercial section with Rightmove Commercial, Realla, NovaLoca and LoopNet.
Pick your power level and click Search with AI. Deal Pro searches every site you've ticked for listings that are live right now, and you can watch each site's progress as it goes.
Scout usually takes under a minute. Expert is a deep search: it goes back to every site round after round, from different angles, then opens the best listings to check they're still available. That can take ten to thirty minutes, and you're free to leave the page and come back to it.

**On screen:** Open **Deal Finder**. Set Rent, 1 bedroom, a price range, type a location. Scroll to the site checkboxes, then the Commercial group. Choose a level, click **Search with AI**. Show the per-site progress list ("round 2 of 8" on Expert).

---

## 5. Your results (1:50)

**Voiceover:**
Your results appear as a sheet. Every row shows the site, the listing, the type, bedrooms, area, postcode and price, with a link to open the original listing.
At the top, the AI shows its top picks. Each pick has a score out of a hundred, a short reason, and an estimate of the monthly profit at eighty per cent occupancy. On Expert, it also adds compliance notes and things to check with the landlord.
Click "Analyse deal" on any pick to send it straight to the analyser. Or tick several rows and click "Analyse selected". You can switch between the sheet and cards, sort by price or bedrooms, and click "Copy to Google Sheets" to paste your results into a spreadsheet.

**On screen:** Scroll the sheet, hover a row and its **Open ↗** link. Scroll up to the top-picks box, hover a score. Tick two rows, hover **Analyse selected**. Click **Cards**, then **Sheet**. Click **Copy to Google Sheets**.

---

## 6. Analysing a deal (2:25)

**Voiceover:**
In the Deal Analyser, paste an advert, a landlord's message or your own notes. The more you include, like the rent, deposit, area and expected nightly rate, the better the answer.
Choose a power level and click Analyse with AI.
You'll get a clear verdict and a score, the likely monthly profit, the upfront cash you'll need, and your break-even occupancy. You'll see the profit at sixty, eighty and a hundred per cent occupancy, the assumptions it made, the key risks, anything that's still missing, and your next steps. On Expert, it also stress-tests the deal: a lower nightly rate, lower occupancy, higher costs and an empty month.
Deal Pro never simply guesses. If it has to estimate something, it tells you.

**On screen:** Click into the text box and paste a sample advert. Choose **Analyst**, click **Analyse with AI**. Scroll through the result: verdict and score, the three figures, occupancy scenarios, risks, missing information, next steps.

---

## 7. Due diligence, saving and deal packs (3:00)

**Voiceover:**
Click "Show advanced controls" to work through the full model. Check the numbers unit by unit, tick off the due diligence checks, like licensing, planning, landlord consent and insurance, and generate a deal pack. Click Save to keep the deal in My deals, so you can come back to it any time.

**On screen:** Click **Show advanced controls**. Click the **Due diligence** step, then **Deal pack**. Click **Save**. Open **My deals** to show the saved deal.

---

## 8. Deal Community (3:15)

**Voiceover:**
In the Deal Community, you can post deals where you've already spoken to the landlord or agent, find deals other sourcers have contacted, reply to posts, and chat with sourcers in your region. It has its own short tutorial at the top of the page.

**On screen:** Open **Deal Community**. Scroll past a few deal cards, hover **Post a deal** and the region chat.

---

## 9. Plans, credits and refunds (3:25)

**Voiceover:**
The Free plan gives you three Scout searches a week and two Scout analyses. Premium, at thirty-nine pounds a month, gives you sixty credits a week and every power level. Max gives you three hundred or twelve hundred credits a week, for sourcers who work deals all day. Credits reset every week.
You can cancel any time. And if Deal Pro isn't right for you, you can request a refund within fourteen days, from the Refunds section on the Plans page.

**On screen:** Open **Plans**. Hover Free, Premium and Max (switch between 5x and 20x). Scroll to the **Refunds** section.

---

## 10. Close (3:45)

**Voiceover:**
And remember, Deal Pro supports your decisions, but it doesn't replace professional advice. Always check legal, planning, licensing and financial information independently before you commit to a deal. Happy sourcing.

**On screen:** Return to **Home** and scroll to the footer disclaimer.
