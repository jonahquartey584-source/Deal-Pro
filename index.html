    Strategy to analyse for

    Power level

    You'll get a clear verdict, and anything that couldn't be verified is shown clearly.

    Your analysis will appear here

    One clear verdict, the important numbers, risks and your next actions.

    Numbers

    Monthly model per unit at 60%, 80% and 100% occupancy, with break-even, lower-rate sensitivity and the London 90-night check.

    Deal

    Units

    Import from an advert

    Paste a landlord or sourcing advert. Deal Pro pulls out the rent, deposit, nightly rate and area as a new unit.

    Assumptions

    30-day month. Other costs cover insurance, software, consumables and minor repairs per unit. Rent is treated as bills-included.

    Due diligence

    Track every check before a deal goes to investors. Run research to draft findings from public sources with links, attach your own evidence, then confirm each check yourself.

    Research power level

    Deal pack

    A pre-NDA investor summary built from your numbers and due diligence. The exact address and landlord stay hidden until the investor signs your terms.

    Pack settings

    Listing photos

    Tap a photo to include or leave it out. Photos of the outside can let an investor find the advert, so consider leaving them out of the pre-NDA version.

    Only send packs through a sourcer who is in a redress scheme, or once you are in one yourself. Keep the numbers labelled as illustrative.

    In the full version this exports a branded PDF. In the demo, generating removes the preview watermark.

    Send to sourcer

    Pass a deal to a compliant sourcer under your Deal Introduction Agreement. The Deal Notice registers the deal so your share is protected.

    3. Sourcer03
      Sells to investorUnder their redress, terms and fee.
    4. Sourcer04
      Pays your shareWithin 7 days of the investor paying them.
